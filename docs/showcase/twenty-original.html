Provan · engineering acceptance

Follow shared chats only when taking part in them, and track what members read

Opening a shared chat no longer follows it or puts it in inbox, while opening still establishes read tracking. Participation and filing actions follow the chat. The optional local adoption of the 2.47 cleanup migration remains an owner decision.

Next: Record the local owner's migration decision. Independently verify runtime criteria r1–r6 in a complete local Twenty environment; if migration is adopted, also execute controlled up/down checks for r7–r8.

Applicationtwentyhq/twenty — The open alternative to Salesforce, designed for AI.
What exists todayThe pre-change behavior coupled access/opening with inbox participation: a missing participant state was treated as followed, so chats a member could merely open could appear in inbox and unread summaries.
Who is affectedTwenty CRM members who can access shared chats, especially readers who have not otherwise participated, and the local owner deciding migration adoption.
Why it mattersThe change prevents shared chats from entering a member's inbox merely because they were opened, without losing cross-chat read tracking. The migration can also remove genuine historic subscriptions because storage cannot distinguish them from reader-only rows.
