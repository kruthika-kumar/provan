# Session 12 public-safe work order

Contract Foundry turns a validated Session 10 Change Brief into a bounded proposal for the existing Session 11 owner-disposition lifecycle. It never creates owner, runtime, policy, verifier, challenge, or execution authority.

The implementation is source-only and target-read-only. Development maturity is `IMPLEMENTED_UNQUALIFIED`; only Gate 12 may qualify Standard or Deep. Package `0.5.0` remains unpublished and extension API major `1` is preserved.

The public work order freezes low-risk, verification-surface, consequential multi-issue, and internal-dogfood case categories. Exact hidden inputs, adjudication, witnesses, reserve cases, private oracles, confidential planning authority, and scanner inputs remain outside Community Git.

The deterministic Standard sequence is blind intent, goal/obstacle analysis, bounded pre-mortem, proposal, adversarial audit, at most one revision, witnesses, verification-pattern selection, then readiness. Deep uses two isolated blind paths, freezes both before synthesis, and permits at most two revisions.

Readiness describes whether a proposed contract and evidence/oracle plan are ready for owner consideration. Eligibility describes whether the requested run could be executed under its required roles. Neither establishes evidence or acceptance.

The budget hard cap is US$75. Optional diversity/research is cut before Standard, controlled comparisons, required real use, or final dogfood.
